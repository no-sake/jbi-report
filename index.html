Why JBI Report

Financial data that
standard databases
can't reach.

Over 99% of Japanese companies are privately held and are not required to publish detailed financial statements. Standard database services can only access what is publicly filed — which for most SMEs is very little.

JBI Report goes further. Drawing on 12+ years of hands-on Japan–NZ trade experience and a background in Japanese corporate banking, we source financial data from non-obvious public channels, interpret it with a lender's eye, and deliver a clear verdict in plain English.

The result is the kind of financial intelligence that gives any company, anywhere in the world, a genuine advantage before signing contracts or committing to large orders with Japanese suppliers.

  • Private company financial data sourced from non-obvious Japanese public channels
  • Interpreted using Japanese corporate banking and credit analysis methodology
  • Benchmarked against METI industry data for context that generic reports lack
  • Delivered in plain English with a clear recommendation — not just raw data

What's included

Private company data.
One clear verdict.

I

Company Profile

Legal name, registration number, registered address, establishment date, legal form, and principal business activities — verified against official Japanese government registries.

II

Financial Health Score

Financial data sourced from non-obvious Japanese public channels — including official gazette filings and government registries that standard databases overlook. Interpreted using Japanese corporate banking methodology.

III

Director & Ownership

Current directors, their appointment dates, and shareholder structure where available — important signals for stability, succession risk, and decision-making authority.

IV

Trade Risk Assessment

Buyer-perspective risk evaluation covering payment culture, export readiness, minimum order realities, and red flags specific to international trade relationships with Japanese suppliers.

V

Negative Flag Check

Screening against insolvency filings, dissolution notices, court records, and regulatory actions using publicly available Japanese government sources.

VI

Recommendation

A clear, plain-English verdict: proceed, proceed with conditions, or hold — with specific notes on what to watch, what to negotiate, and what questions to ask before signing.
